Netram System Architecture Flow Cards

Interconnected modular overview designed for executive presentations and technical slides

Card 1: Edge & Ingestion
📱 Field Ingestion
On-site mobile field inspections with offline-first SQLite queues, geotagging, photo SHA-256 integrity, and edge CCTV feeds.
Connected To
  • ➡️ Core API: Idempotent sync of offline batches
  • ➡️ MinIO S3: Direct pre-signed photo upload
  • ➡️ CCTV Gateway: Local RTSP stream push/pull
Protocols Used
HTTPS REST S3 PUT RTSP SQLite
Card 2: Security & Gateway
⚙️ Core Platform Hub
Fastify Modular Monolith enforcing RBAC, Jurisdiction scopes, CCTV stream token minting, and the Transactional Outbox pattern.
Connected To
  • ⬅️ Card 1 (Field): Reconciles sync batches
  • ➡️ Card 3 (Vault): Atomic DB transactions & AI
  • ➡️ Card 4 (Control): Management APIs & WHEP
Protocols Used
JWT / JOSE Redis Pub/Sub Auth Hooks
Card 3: Data & Storage
🗄️ Storage & Intelligence
PostgreSQL 16 relational data, MinIO encrypted media storage, and FastAPI advisory AI anomaly detection engine.
Connected To
  • ⬅️ Card 2 (API): Serves queries & BullMQ jobs
  • ➡️ Card 4 (Control): Supplies audit & evidence
Protocols Used
Drizzle ORM MinIO S3 FastAPI
Card 4: Presentation
🖥️ Command & Oversight
Unified Next.js 15 Web platform for State/District control rooms, live CCTV multi-viewers, and public grievance tracking.
Connected To
  • ⬅️ Card 2 (API): Portal dashboards & actions
  • ⬅️ Gateway: Sub-second WebRTC video feeds
  • ⬅️ Realtime: Live push notifications
Protocols Used
WebSockets WebRTC (WHEP) HLS
